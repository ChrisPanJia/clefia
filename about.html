Our Story

Nurturing musical talent since 1995

Clefia Academy of Music

At Clefia Academy of Music, we believe that music is more than a discipline — it is a way of life. It is emotion made audible, imagination set free.

Founded in the vibrant heart of Chatswood, Clefia was born from a vision to create a music academy where technical excellence meets artistic soul, where every student is treated not just as a learner, but as a unique storyteller through sound.

Our community brings together elite performers, passionate educators, and curious minds — all bound by a shared belief that music has the power to transform lives.

Our Mission

To cultivate not just skilled musicians, but expressive artists — confident, curious, and connected — through inspired teaching, meaningful performance, and a love for music that lasts a lifetime.

Our Vision

To become a leading institution of music education in Australia, where tradition and innovation walk hand in hand, and where every student’s voice — in music and in life — is heard, nurtured, and celebrated.

Our Core Values

Excellence

We believe in striving for brilliance — in every note played, every lesson delivered, and every concert performed. Our faculty sets the highest standards, guiding each student with world-class expertise and unwavering dedication

Passion

We teach because we love. Passion fuels our classrooms, our rehearsals, our concerts. It ignites creativity, sustains perseverance, and transforms practice into purpose

Community

Music is meant to be shared. At Clefia, we build more than skills — we build belonging. Through ensemble work, musical theatre, festivals, and collaborations, our students grow alongside one another in joy and solidarity

Innovation

We embrace a modern vision of music education. From integrating drama, visual storytelling and movement, to culturally diverse repertoire and bilingual support — our programs are as dynamic as the world we live in
