Global Audition Support Program

Individually Tailored. Globally Recognised. Exceptionally Delivered.

Your Pathway to the World’s Leading Music Institutions

At Clefia Academy of Music, we don’t just prepare students for auditions — we prepare them for global stages.

Whether you dream of Juilliard, Royal College of Music, Paris Conservatoire, or Sydney Con, our Global Audition Support Program provides world-class, highly personalized, and strategically structured preparation to help aspiring musicians achieve their highest artistic and academic goals.

From undergraduate and postgraduate conservatory applications, to pre-college divisions, master programs, summer intensives, and even professional orchestral and operatic auditions, Clefia’s program is designed for success on a global scale.

Program Highlights

1. Strategic Repertoire Curation

We work with each student to develop a tailored audition repertoire that aligns with the artistic expectations and technical standards of their target institutions — balancing classical depth with personal expression.

2. Mock Auditions & Performance Psychology

  • Stage presence & anxiety management
  • Timing, transitions & program flow
  • Jury psychology: how to stand out in 10 minutes

3. Audition Video Portfolio Production

From video framing and lighting to musical takes and attire — we ensure your recorded audition is polished, compelling, and competitive.

4. Application & Interview Mentorship

  • Personal statement writing & music CVs
  • Recommendation letter strategies
  • Scholarship applications
  • Audition interview coaching (Zoom/in-person)

5. Music Theory & Aural Intensive (Optional)

Targeted support for entrance theory and musicianship tests required by top institutions like RCM, Juilliard, Mozarteum, etc.

Meet Our Global Mentors

Christian Qi – International Baritone & Mentor

Jack Cheng – Australia’s Only Steinway Artist

Renowned for his expressive interpretation and virtuosic control, Jack has performed and taught worldwide. His students have gone on to top conservatories and achieved success in elite competitions and international programs.

Dr. Shufang Zhang – Concert Pianist & Academic
