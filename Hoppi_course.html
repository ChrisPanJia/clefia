Group Classes

Mini Hoppi (Ages 3–5)

Where music begins — through laughter, movement, and imagination.

Mini Hoppi is Clefia’s early childhood music discovery program. Children sing, clap, play, and explore music through solfège, storytelling, rhythm games, puppets, and percussive instruments — all in a bilingual setting.

Your child will gain:

  • Fundamental pitch and rhythm sense
  • Early solfège and music literacy
  • Emotional expression through music
  • Confidence and group interaction
  • A joyful, creative relationship with sound

Learning music begins with feeling music.
