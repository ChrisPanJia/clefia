Clefia Contemporary Music Program

Create. Perform. Produce. Your Sound Starts Here.

The Clefia Contemporary Music Program is not just a set of classes — it’s a launchpad for the next generation of music creators. Designed for students aged 10+, this program blends performance, songwriting, composition, and digital music production into a comprehensive creative journey.

Whether your child dreams of becoming a singer-songwriter, film composer, pop artist, producer, or simply wants to express themselves through music, Clefia offers the tools, mentorship, and performance experiences to bring those dreams to life.

What Students Learn

Pop & Contemporary Vocal Performance

  • Learn safe, expressive vocal technique for pop, R&B, musical theatre & crossover genres
  • Develop microphone technique, stage presence, and interpretation
  • Master modern vocal styles: riffing, vocal textures, storytelling through voice
  • Explore harmony singing & ensemble performance

Instrumental Performance (Pop Piano, Guitar, Bass, Drums)

  • Learn to read chord charts, lead sheets, and groove in real-world settings
  • Accompany singers, lead a band, improvise within a style
  • Study rhythm, phrasing, and genre-specific articulation
  • Build musicianship through ensemble rehearsal and live gigs

Songwriting & Lyric Craft

  • Compose original melodies and write meaningful lyrics
  • Understand form, hooks, rhyme, and emotional structure
  • Analyse songs by famous artists and create in your own voice
  • Build a personal songwriting portfolio

Music Production & Technology

  • Learn how music is made in today’s digital world
  • Use professional tools like Logic Pro X, GarageBand, or Ableton Live
  • Create beats, layer instruments, record vocals, and shape sound
  • Learn audio mixing, effects, sampling, and creative sound design
  • Bring a song from rough idea to produced demo

Composition for Visual Media (Intermediate–Advanced)

  • Learn to compose music for short films, dance, games, and visual storytelling
  • Understand emotional scoring, atmosphere, and transitions
  • Sync audio to picture and create cinematic soundscapes
  • Ideal for students interested in future careers in film/game music

Band Coaching & Live Ensemble

  • Work collaboratively in bands or small groups
  • Arrange popular songs or originals
  • Practice rehearsal strategies, live set design, and audience communication
  • Regular performances at Clefia concerts, showcases, and pop-up events

Pathways & Achievements

  • AMEB Rockschool Exams – structured achievement in voice, keys, drums, guitar, composition
  • Performance opportunities – school festivals, showcases, original song premieres
  • Music Portfolio development – ideal for HSC Music Extension, arts high school entry, or scholarships
  • Original songs recorded & performed — with support from Clefia’s in-house producers & engineers
  • Entry into cross-disciplinary programs — collaborate with Clefia’s drama, musical theatre, or classical divisions

Why Clefia’s Program Stands Out

  • Designed by working professionals in performance, songwriting & production
  • Multilingual instruction available
  • Integrated with technology — students learn both traditional skills and modern tools
  • Creativity-first philosophy — every child’s voice, taste, and identity are respected
  • Flexible levels — whether you’re starting out or preparing to release original work

Who Is This For?

  • Children & teens aged 9+ who love modern music and want to go beyond lessons
  • Classical students wanting to branch into songwriting, pop, or production
  • Creatives who write songs, produce beats, or want to learn how
  • HSC Music students, AMEB Rockschool candidates, or music scholarship applicants
  • Any young person who wants to explore, create, and grow in a safe, exciting, and professional environment
