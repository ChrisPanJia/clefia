AMEB Music Theory Class

Fast-track your understanding of music

Clefia’s intensive theory program is designed for students preparing for AMEB Grade 1–3. This course transforms dry rules into dynamic, visual, and engaging lessons.

What’s included

  • Interactive instruction on rhythm, notation, keys, intervals, harmony
  • Creative games, worksheets, mock exams
  • Visual storytelling + memory techniques
  • Small groups, high attention
  • Multilingual (English, Chinese & Korean) support

From pen to performance, theory becomes a language students truly understand.
