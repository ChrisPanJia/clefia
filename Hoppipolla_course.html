Hoppipolla – Children’s Musical Theatre Program (Ages 6–9)

Sing. Act. Move. Shine.

Hoppipolla is Clefia Academy’s signature group program designed for children aged 6–9, combining the magic of music, drama, and movement into an unforgettable creative experience.

More than just a class — Hoppipolla is a stage for imagination, where children build confidence, express emotion, and tell powerful stories through song and performance. Each term, students rehearse and present an original or adapted musical showcase, developing their skills in:

Course Highlights

  • Vocal Performance: Learn healthy singing technique, breathing, articulation, pitch control, and expressive phrasing — all through fun, engaging songs children love.
  • Acting & Character Work: Explore facial expression, storytelling, body language, and improvisation. Children learn to embody roles, collaborate with peers, and bring characters to life.
  • Movement & Stagecraft: Choreographed movement builds rhythm, coordination, and stage awareness. Students gain confidence in using space and performing for an audience.
  • Performance Projects: Every term ends with a live musical theatre performance — complete with costumes, props, lighting, and audience. Children shine on stage, building courage and pride.
  • Holistic Learning: Along the way, students naturally develop language skills, musicality, creativity, social-emotional awareness, and teamwork — all in a safe, joyful environment.

What Makes Hoppipolla Different?

  • Led by professional performers and music educators with real stage experience
  • Multilingual environment available, supporting diverse families
  • Original productions and curated repertoire aligned with age-appropriate themes
  • Integrated with Clefia’s concert & festival schedule, giving students regular stage experience
  • Builds a pathway from early creativity to formal voice, piano, drama, or AMEB training
